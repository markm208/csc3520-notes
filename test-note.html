Built-in Notes Features

Every section of this page has an "Add note" button after it. Hover over the button and you will see the section your note will attach to light up in blue.

Click the button, type your note, and save it. Try it on this paragraph.

Your notes can use basic markdown:

  • bold content surrounded by a pair of with double asterisks
  • italics content surrounded by a pair of with single asterisks
  • code with backticks (the key above the Tab key).

If something in lecture does not make sense, check the box that says "I need clarification from the instructor" when you write your note.

It turns the note red so it stands out when you review.

I cannot see these notes, so if you want an answer, bring the question to class or office hours. The red flag is a reminder for you, not a message to me.

Where Your Notes Are Stored (Read This Part Carefully)

Your notes are stored inside your web browser on your computer, in something called local storage:

  • Your notes are private. They never leave your computer. I cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.
  • Your notes live in one browser on one machine. If you take notes in Chrome on your laptop, they will not appear in Firefox.
  • If you clear your browser data, your notes are deleted with it.

Backing Up Your Notes

The floating toolbar in the bottom corner has a backup button. It gives you two options:

  • Export notes to a file. This downloads a small file containing your notes for this page. Keep it wherever you keep your coursework.
  • Import notes from a file. This loads a backup into the browser you are using. This is how you move your notes to another computer, or recover after clearing browser data.

There is also a download button in the toolbar that saves a copy of the whole page with your notes written into it.

That copy is good for offline reading and printing, but it cannot be imported back. The backup file is what you use to restore notes.

The Rest of the Toolbar

  • The list icon opens a table of contents. Sections where you have notes show a count.
  • The plus and minus buttons change the text size. The page remembers your choice.
  • The notes toggle expands or collapses all of your notes at once.
  • The printer icon prints the page or saves it as a PDF, with your notes included.
  • The question mark icon cycles through any questions on the page.
  • The info button reminds you of how storage works and shows how much space your notes use.

One More Thing About Edits

Sometimes I fix a typo or improve a section and repost a page. If a section you annotated changes, your note is not deleted. It moves to a "Notes from removed or changed sections" area at the bottom of the page, along with the original text it referred to, so you can decide what to do with it.
